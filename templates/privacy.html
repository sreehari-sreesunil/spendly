Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical data, such as browser type, to keep the service working properly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate and improve Spendly: to authenticate you, to record and summarise your expenses, and to provide support. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database on the servers that run Spendly. Passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text. We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
